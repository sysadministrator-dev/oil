# **App Name**: МаслоГуру

## Core Features:

- Product Showcase: A visually appealing section displaying various types of car oils with their key characteristics and benefits.
- Detailed Product View: Users can click on any oil to reveal more in-depth specifications, usage recommendations, and a 'buy now' or 'inquire' call to action.
- AI-Powered Oil Selector Tool: A generative AI tool that guides users in selecting the optimal engine oil based on their vehicle's make, model, year, and driving conditions by interpreting general input.
- Inquiry & Contact Form: A straightforward form for visitors to ask questions, request more information, or initiate a purchase inquiry, with backend processing for submissions.
- About Us & Brand Information: A dedicated section providing insights into the company's expertise, the quality of the brands offered, and customer service guarantees.

## Style Guidelines:

- The chosen color palette evokes a sense of professionalism, depth, and reliability, fitting for automotive products. Primary color (accenting UI elements): a deep, authoritative Indigo (#1F1F99). The hue creates a strong brand presence. Background color: a dark purplish-blue (#1D1A2E) with reduced saturation to provide a sophisticated, calm foundation. Accent color (for highlights and calls-to-action): a vibrant, clear Blue (#5CBBE6). Its lighter and more saturated nature offers excellent contrast, drawing attention without detracting from the primary focus. The accent's hue is closely related yet distinct enough to create visual interest while maintaining harmony with the primary color.
- Headline and body text font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif. It is chosen for its modern, neutral, and highly readable characteristics, suitable for conveying product information and building trust.
- Clean, modern line-art icons that visually represent aspects like vehicle types, oil specifications, delivery, and customer support, maintaining a professional aesthetic.
- A single-page, section-based layout with smooth vertical navigation. Key sections will include a prominent hero, a detailed product catalog, an interactive AI oil selector tool, an 'About Us' statement, and a clear contact form, all designed for an intuitive user journey.
- Subtle, understated animations on scroll to introduce new content sections, along with gentle hover effects on interactive elements such as product cards and buttons, enhancing user engagement without being distracting.
